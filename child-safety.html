Junto child safety standards

Junto is for people aged 18 and older. Child sexual abuse and exploitation are prohibited on Junto.

Prohibited content and conduct

Do not create, upload, request, distribute or promote child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual exploitation of children, grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, trafficking, or content that sexualises or endangers a child. These rules apply to photos, captions, profiles and all interactions on Junto.

Report a concern

Use Report in the app’s post or user menu. You can also use Settings → Email support, or email emma.vandewouwer@gmail.com, Junto’s monitored child-safety contact.

Include the relevant username, approximate time and a description that helps locate the activity. Do not download, forward or attach suspected illegal images. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Safety action

Junto can remove prohibited content and restrict or terminate accounts. Automated content checks help prevent harmful uploads; they do not replace reporting or assessment of a concern. Confirmed child sexual abuse material must be removed and reported to the relevant authority where required by applicable law.

Junto’s safety contact handles child-safety concerns and requests from the relevant authorities. Blocking another user is also available in the app.
