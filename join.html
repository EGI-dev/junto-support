Android testing

Junto is currently available through Google Play testing to invited, eligible testers aged 18 and older. An invitation from a friend does not automatically enrol you.

Open the internal testing invitation. This link works only for accounts authorised for that test. If you are joining a closed test, use the separate opt-in link supplied by the organiser.
