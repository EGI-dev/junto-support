Junto support

Delete your Junto account

You can delete your account in Junto, or request deletion by email without installing or opening the app.

Request deletion without the app

Email emma.vandewouwer@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Junto account. Send the request from your registered account email and include your Junto username so we can identify the account.

We may ask you to verify ownership before deletion. If you cannot access your registered email, explain this in your request so support can help verify ownership. Do not send your password or verification codes.

Delete in the app

Sign in, open Settings → Delete Account, and confirm. Account deletion is permanent.

What is deleted

The deletion flow removes your sign-in account, profile, owned photos and posts, and associated friendship, reaction, notification and other social records from Junto’s active backend. Device notification targets associated with the account are removed with it. Other people’s accounts and their independently saved copies are not deleted.

Retention and other privacy requests

Post expiry only ends visibility; it is not a promise that every stored copy is erased immediately. Provider logs, backups, and records subject to a lawful safety or legal retention requirement can have separate retention periods. Contact support for the applicable purpose and period, or to request deletion of analytics or other data without closing your account. We will explain any information that cannot be deleted and why.
