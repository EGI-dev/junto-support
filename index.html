Junto support

Help with your account, sharing a photo, and keeping your experience safe.

Signing in

Use your Junto username or email and password. If your email has not been verified, complete the email-code step. Use Forgot Password? if you need to reset your password.

If Junto reports a connection or service problem, keep your details and try again later. Creating another account will not resolve a service outage.

Posting a photo

Take a photo, write a caption, choose how long your post remains visible, and attach a valid place or location. Use nearby venue search or the manual venue option. You can tag friends who are with you.

Photos and captions are checked for harmful content before publication. The updated app asks permission to share this content with OpenAI for that safety check. Cancelling keeps your draft.

Android privacy

Read the Android privacy information for nearby-place providers, optional analytics, contacts and notification processing.

Notifications

Enable Junto notifications in your phone’s Settings (Android or iPhone). Organisers receive an update when someone joins them. Friends who are not already participating may receive an invitation to join, subject to friendship and privacy settings.

Report content or block someone

Use the options on a post to report inappropriate content. Blocking and visibility controls are available in the app. If you need further help, contact support with the relevant username and a description; never send your password or verification code.

Delete your account

Open Junto’s Settings and choose Delete Account. Confirm only if you want to remove your account and associated app data. A post’s visibility timer is separate from account deletion. You can also request account deletion without the app.
